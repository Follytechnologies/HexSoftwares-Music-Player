## 🚀 Features

* ✅ Playlist display with track title, artist, and duration
* ✅ Play and pause the currently selected song
* ✅ Next button — skip to the next track
* ✅ Previous button — restart track if over 3s played, else go back
* ✅ Real-time progress bar with elapsed and total time display
* ✅ Click-to-seek — click anywhere on the progress bar to jump
* ✅ Volume control slider with live visual fill
* ✅ Auto-play next track when current one ends
* ✅ Animated equalizer visualizer while playing
* ✅ Per-track colour-themed album art
* ✅ Decorative waveform at the bottom of the playlist panel
* ✅ Fully responsive design

## 🛠️ How It Works

**Playing a Track**
- Click any track in the playlist on the right panel to select it
- Click the cyan circular **Play** button to start playback

**Playback Controls**
- Click **Play/Pause** to toggle playback
- Click **Next** to skip to the next song
- Click **Previous** to restart the current track (if over 3 seconds in) or go to the previous track

**Seeking**
- Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that position in the track

**Volume**
- Drag the volume slider left or right to adjust the audio level
